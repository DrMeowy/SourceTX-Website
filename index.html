before your first drive

Set up safely, then enjoy the drive.

Take a moment to check your controls and link before applying drive power. Always test with the vehicle restrained or its wheels off the ground.

Open the installation guide →
  1. 01
    Connect your transmitter.

    Use the companion app or browser tools to install SourceTX and choose your model.

  2. 02
    Check your controls.

    Center the steering and throttle, confirm their direction, and check the receiver link.

  3. 03
    Test before driving.

    Keep the vehicle restrained, keep throttle ready to stop, and stop if the controls or link behave unexpectedly.
