Surface RC Engineering

Why RC Drivers Choose SourceTX

Engineered specifically for land vehicles: RC cars, rock crawlers, drift chassis, monster trucks, dual-motor rovers, and surface robotics.

Sub-Millisecond Surface Response

Hardware-backed ADC sampling and CRSF single-wire UART transport for ExpressLRS & Crossfire surface receivers with auto-negotiation up to 1.87M baud.

Crawler & 4WS Mixers

Built-in surface mixers: 4-Wheel Steering (Front, Rear, Crab, Circle), 2-Speed Transmission shift, Front/Rear Diff Locks, Winch control, and Dual-ESC tank skid steering.

Dynamic Hardware Pin Mapping

Re-assign any physical GPIO directly over USB without recompiling: Steering pot/hall sensor, Throttle trigger, multi-position switches, RGB status LED, and buzzer.

Windows & Android Companions

Native companion apps for Windows (.NET WPF) and Android (Jetpack Compose) for USB OTG flashing, live model profiles, steering expo curves, and hardware calibration.

ABS Braking & Steering Expo Curves

Fine-tune exponential steering response, dual-rates, throttle deadbands, punch control, and anti-lock pulse braking for extreme track or drift control.

ESC Telemetry & Haptic Alarms

Monitor real-time ESC voltage, motor RPM, and signal strength with customizable audio buzzer alert tones and haptic vibration feedback for low battery.

Getting Started

Flash & Drive in 3 Simple Steps

01

Connect & Flash

Plug your ESP32-S3 transmitter board into your PC or Android phone via USB OTG. Open the Companion App and press Install SourceTX.

02

Wire Wheel & Trigger

Connect your steering wheel pot, throttle trigger, and auxiliary shift switches to the ADC/GPIO pins, and hook up the single-wire CRSF line.

03

Bind & Drive!

Power on your RC car, crawler, or truck. SourceTX automatically establishes the ultra-low-latency CRSF link with full telemetry!
