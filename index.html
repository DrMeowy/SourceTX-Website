Engineered for Performance

Why Pilots Choose SourceTX

Built from the ground up for microsecond response times, rock-solid failsafe protection, and complete hardware freedom.

Protected CRSF Transport

Hardware-backed UART single-wire transport for ExpressLRS & Crossfire modules with auto-baud negotiation up to 1.87M baud and sub-millisecond packet delivery.

ESP32-S3 Dual-Core Power

Dedicated FreeRTOS core for input sampling and RF transmission, running independently from the OLED display, audio engine, and telemetry parser.

Dynamic Hardware Pin Mapping

Re-assign any physical GPIO directly over USB without recompiling: CRSF TX/RX, RGB WS2812 status LED, passive buzzer, DFPlayer audio, and vibration motor.

Cross-Platform Companion Apps

Native apps for Windows (.NET WPF) and Android (Jetpack Compose) for instant USB OTG firmware flashing, verified updates, and live pin configuration.

Cryptographic Flash Verification

Every firmware install and update is verified directly on-chip with hardware SHA-256 and SPI MD5 digest checking before boot.

Smart Audio & Haptic Feedback

Custom audio buzzer alarms, voice alerts via DFPlayer Mini, and haptic vibration for low battery warnings, link loss, and timer milestones.

Getting Started

Flash & Fly in 3 Simple Steps

01

Connect & Flash

Plug your ESP32-S3 board into your PC or Android phone via USB OTG. Open the Companion App and press Install SourceTX.

02

Wire Your Hardware

Connect your ExpressLRS transmitter module to the CRSF single-wire GPIO pin and wire your gimbal potentiometers to the ADC pins.

03

Bind & Fly

Power on your drone or model. SourceTX automatically establishes the ultra-low-latency CRSF link with full bidirectional telemetry!
