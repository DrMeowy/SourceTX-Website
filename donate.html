where support goes

Small contributions fund practical work.

There is no paid product tier or required account. Support is optional and goes toward keeping the project usable, testable, and documented.

01

Test hardware

Boards, displays, touch controllers, batteries, and replacement parts for repeatable checks.

02

Radio and receivers

ExpressLRS modules, antennas, receivers, and safe loads for link and telemetry testing.

03

Lab tools

USB recovery, measurement, wiring, and debugging equipment that shortens the feedback loop.

04

Hosting and time

Release hosting, documentation, companion maintenance, and the time needed to keep the project coherent.
